# Cyro pricing

Cyro's paying customer market is **Ghana**. The product, research engine and knowledgebase are **not limited to Ghana**: users can research Ghana, Africa, or anywhere in the world.

## Plans

| Plan | Daily | Yearly* | Position |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Essential | ₵0.50/day | ₵182.50/year | Everyday research |
| Research | ₵1.00/day | ₵365.00/year | More frequent research and saved knowledge |
| Deep Research | ₵2.00/day | ₵730.00/year | Heavier research workloads |

*Yearly prices currently represent 365 × the daily rate. No annual discount is assumed until one is explicitly configured.

Customers can choose **daily or yearly billing**. Payment-provider integration is separate from product pricing so Cyro can support Ghanaian payment rails first and other payment methods later.

The ₵0.50/day Essential plan is the minimum paid access level, not a free-trial price.

Prices are product configuration and may change before public launch.
